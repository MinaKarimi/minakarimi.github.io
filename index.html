About

From geomatics to GeoAI

My research lies at the intersection of geospatial information science, spatial data science, and artificial intelligence. My work has evolved from geospatial information systems and augmented reality toward machine learning for geographic text, place representation, and, more recently, GeoAI, large language models, geographic diversity, and AI alignment.

I am currently a University Assistant Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Geography and Regional Research at the University of Vienna. I received my PhD in Geospatial Information Systems Engineering from K. N. Toosi University of Technology and have conducted research in Switzerland at the University of Zurich and FHNW.
