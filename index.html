From Geomatics to GeoAI

From Geomatics to GeoAI

My academic journey began in Geomatics Engineering and Geographic Information Systems, where I worked on spatial databases, web mapping, ubiquitous GIS, and augmented reality for underground infrastructure. During my doctoral research, I moved toward machine learning and natural language processing to investigate how textual content can reveal the functionality and meaning of places.

Today, my research connects this geospatial foundation with GeoAI and foundation models. I study how AI systems represent geographic concepts and places, how those representations vary across regions and cultures, and how we can evaluate and improve them through knowledge representation, representation learning, geographic diversity, and alignment. This work brings together GIScience, spatial data science, AI ethics, and the emerging study of geographic perspectives encoded in large language and multimodal models.

Research interests

GIScienceSpatial Data ScienceGeoAIAI EthicsFoundation ModelsLLMsAlignmentKnowledge Representation

Geographic Representation & Bias

How AI systems represent places, regions, cultures, and geographic concepts—and where those representations become uneven or stereotypical.

GeoAI & Foundation Models

Geospatial applications of large language and multimodal models, with an emphasis on spatial knowledge and reasoning.

Geo-Alignment & AI Ethics

Understanding geographic misalignment, diversity, and fairness as core questions in the evaluation of AI systems.

Knowledge Representation

Geographic concepts, cognitive regions, semantic structures, and the ways AI encodes knowledge about space and place.

Representation Learning

Machine learning methods for extracting spatial and functional structures from textual and geospatial data.

Place & Spatial Knowledge

Place functionality, sense of place, spatial semantics, and human-centered geographic representations.
